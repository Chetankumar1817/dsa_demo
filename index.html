Stack (LIFO)

A stack is like a pile of plates: you only add or remove at the top. The last item in is the first item out (Last In, First Out).

Push adds to the top. Pop removes the top. Peek looks at the top without removing it.

    Queue (FIFO)

    A queue is like a line at a shop: people join at the rear and leave from the front. The first item in is the first item out (First In, First Out).

    Enqueue adds at the rear. Dequeue removes from the front.

    Items leave on the FRONT side (left) and join at the REAR side (right).

      Tower of Hanoi

      Move the whole tower from peg A to peg C.

      1. Move only one disk at a time.
      2. Only the top disk of a peg can be moved.
      3. A larger disk can never sit on a smaller one.

      The best solution needs 2n − 1 moves for n disks.

      Moves: 0Minimum: 7

        Singly Linked List

        A linked list is a chain of nodes. Each node holds a value and a link (arrow) to the next node. The HEAD points at the first node and the last node points to null (nothing).

        To find a value you must start at the head and follow the arrows one by one.
